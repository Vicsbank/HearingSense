GuitarMood

AI-Powered Music Emotion and Genre Recognition System

Utilizing deep learning technology to analyze emotional features in guitar audio, intelligently identifying nine emotional dimensions, and classifying the music genre from its Mel spectrogram.

Amazement Solemnity Tenderness Nostalgia Calmness Power Joyful Activation Tension Sadness
🎵

Audio Emotion Recognition

Using a CNN deep learning model to analyze guitar audio, extracting Mel spectrogram features to accurately identify nine emotional dimensions with industry-leading accuracy.

🎼

Music Genre Recognition

A CNN + GRU model reads the Mel spectrogram over time and classifies the music into one of ten GTZAN genres.

⚡

Real-time Processing Capability

The FastAPI backend architecture provides high-performance real-time audio processing capabilities, supporting rapid analysis of various audio formats such as MP3 and WAV.

🧠

Deep Learning Engine

A CNN neural network built on the PyTorch framework, trained on a large amount of music emotion data, with powerful feature extraction and classification capabilities.

🎯

Multi-dimensional Emotion Analysis

Supports the simultaneous recognition of nine emotional dimensions, providing a detailed emotional probability distribution to offer a clear picture of the music's emotional character.

🔄

End-to-End Workflow

From audio upload to emotion and genre recognition, results are shown in the browser in a single step.
